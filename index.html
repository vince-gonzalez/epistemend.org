About the Author

Vince Gonzalez

Independent researcher · ORCID 0009-0005-3640-014X

Most of the work asks one question: what does a claim actually rest on, and does the record agree with itself?

It runs from the axioms a formal proof spends — measured from the source rather than assumed — through machine-checked bounds in discrete geometry, to a formal account of testimony and the colour science behind accessible vision tests. The papers are open access and every one carries a DOI; the tools that produced them are public.
