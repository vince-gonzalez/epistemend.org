Vince Gonzalez

Independent researcher · formal methods, discrete geometry, formal epistemology · ORCID 0009-0005-3640-014X

Research

  • Formal methods. Axiom provenance for Lean 4 and Metamath: separating what a theorem states from what its proof consumes, measured across libraries, with the avoidable dependencies removed upstream.
  • Discrete geometry. Certified upper and lower bounds for covering, packing and opacity problems, carried in exact and interval arithmetic so a computed inequality becomes a proof.
  • Formal epistemology. Formal accounts of testimony, attestation and the admissibility of machine-generated evidence; the Modulign dimensional-address standard and its application to law and regulation.
  • Colour-vision methodology. Reproducible pseudoisochromatic plate generation and recovery, with a published off-axis palette limitation reported rather than corrected silently (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.21310578).
