Measurement

What the platform actually does

Documentation describes the limits one at a time and does not say how they interact. These harnesses measure the behaviour from outside and publish the raw data — the reproducible half of the research.

Vince Gonzalez · ORCID 0009-0005-3640-014X

kv-bench

Cross-region staleness in Cloudflare Workers KV: how long a read keeps serving the old value after a write, and how that window scales with the cacheTtl you set. Timed from a clock outside the edge, reproducible, and archived with a DOI.

d1-replica-bench

Read-replica lag in Cloudflare D1: after a primary write, how long a replica in another region can still answer stale. Sub-round-trip in practice — and the finding that replicas are traffic-provisioned, not toggle-provisioned, published alongside the poll-scheduling correction that got there.

locationhint-bench

Where a Durable Object actually lands when you ask for a region. Three of the documented location hints never reach their named region; placement is verified by trace rather than assumed.

d1bench

Which D1 bulk-write strategy wins, and which limit stops you first. A single batch() took 150,000 statements without refusal — the real ceiling is a thirty-second time budget, not a statement count — and literal SQL runs 2.7× faster at 64 columns until a row passes the statement-length limit and it stops being expressible at all. The upstream documentation was corrected from this measurement.

plateaudit

Measuring pseudoisochromatic colour-vision plates from the stimulus a browser actually delivers, rather than the one the generator intended.
